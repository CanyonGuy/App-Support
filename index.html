Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to mobile applications developed and published by Innovation Consulting (“we,” “our,” or “us”).

Information We Collect

This application collects limited personal information solely for the purpose of transmitting your details to authorized Search and Rescue personnel during an emergency.

Specifically, when you initiate a Search and Rescue request, we collect:

  • Your name.
  • Your email address or phone number (as provided by you).
  • Your precise location, including latitude, longitude, and altitude.
  • Only the information necessary to assist Search and Rescue teams.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or any tracking technologies.

How We Use Your Information

The information you provide is used exclusively to support emergency Search and Rescue operations. When you activate an emergency request, your name, contact information, and precise location are transmitted directly to authorized Search and Rescue personnel so they can locate and assist you.

Your information is never used for marketing, analytics, advertising, or any non-emergency purpose. It is not shared with the app developer, stored on developer servers, or transmitted to any third parties outside of Search and Rescue responders.

Data Retention

We do not retain or store any personal information on our servers. All data collected by the application remains on your device and is only transmitted when you initiate a Search and Rescue request.

Once the emergency transmission is completed, no copies of your information are stored, logged, or retained by Innovation Consulting. We do not have access to your data at any time.

Permissions

If the application requests device permissions (such as camera, storage, GPS, or notifications), those permissions are used solely for features within the app and are not used to collect or transmit personal information to us beyond what is required for Search and Rescue operations.

Third-Party Services

This application does not use third-party advertising networks or external analytics services that collect personal user data.

Children’s Privacy

This application does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 except when necessary to support an emergency Search and Rescue request initiated by the user.

Data Security

Data transmitted for Search and Rescue purposes is handled securely and is not stored on our servers beyond what is required to complete the emergency request.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted within the application or accompanying distribution channels.
